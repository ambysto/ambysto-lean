4. How Lean moves your files

Lean changes your files only when you ask it to, and only in the ways listed here. You are responsible for reviewing what you approve before you approve it, and for keeping your own backups.

  • Lean moves items only to the Trash (macOS) or the Recycle Bin (Windows), and only after you approve the move. Lean itself never deletes anything permanently and never empties the Trash or the Recycle Bin.
  • Nothing is selected for you to move. The one exception, on macOS only, is Uninstall, which selects the app you chose to uninstall; its leftovers are not selected. On Windows, Uninstall opens Windows Settings, where you remove the app yourself, and Lean selects nothing.
  • Right before each move, Lean measures the item again and checks that it is allowed, unchanged and not in use. If a check fails, Lean skips the item and tells you why.
  • Every move is written to a journal on your computer, and Put back can restore an item from it. Put back works only while the item is still in the Trash or the Recycle Bin and nothing else has taken its place; if you empty it, Lean cannot restore the item.
  • Disk space returns when you empty the Trash or the Recycle Bin, not when Lean moves an item. Sizes shown before a move are estimates, and moving an item can free less than shown, for example when copies share storage.
  • Lean suggests which copy of a duplicate to keep, and which items are large or old. These are suggestions. You decide what to move.
  • On Windows, the Recycle Bin is managed by Windows, and Windows deletes some items for good instead of keeping them in the Recycle Bin: items on removable drives and network drives, online-only cloud files, items larger than the Recycle Bin's size limit, items when the Recycle Bin is turned off, and items with very long paths. Lean refuses to move such items before it moves anything. If an item still does not reach the Recycle Bin intact, Lean reports "Not found intact in the Recycle Bin", records it in the journal and stops the session. Such an item cannot be put back.
  • An update never turns a destructive option on.
