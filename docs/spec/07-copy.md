# 07 — Words

The UI states what happened, never more.

Windows About (ASP-11): "Free to use. We only charge when you are happy to pay.", "Website", "Check for updates", "Checking for updates…", "You have the latest version.", "Could not check for updates. Try again later.", "Version <version> is available.", "Could not open the download page. Try again later." and "Could not open the website. Try again later.". Donate is hidden in v1. Licence labels retain their existing words, in About instead of Settings.

**Never used in user-facing text:** "junk", "free up", "freed", "cleaned", "clean up your", "boost", "speed up", "optimize", "garbage".

**Required phrasing:**
- After a deletion: "Moved to Trash: <size> (measured just now)." and "Disk space returns when you empty the Trash."
- Purgeable space: "macOS frees this itself when space is needed."
- Estimates are labelled "about" or "estimate" until re-measured.
- Display-only items always say why and where to manage them.
- The two Tidy lists are named **"Empty folders"** and **"Broken shortcuts"**. Those rows never claim disk space.

Windows notes: "Moved to the Recycle Bin: <size> (measured just now)." and "Disk space returns when you empty the Recycle Bin."; File Explorer for Finder; no purgeable line; "Trash" is "Recycle Bin" in every string, Put back reasons included ("No longer in the Recycle Bin (emptied or moved)", "The item in the Recycle Bin is a different file", "Not in a Recycle Bin folder"); a `lost` item: the deletion report says "Not found intact in the Recycle Bin", and the journal reason, the history and Put back say "Windows deleted this permanently instead of moving it to the Recycle Bin" (05); an item in the Recycle Bin that did not match the approved item: "The moved item did not match the approved item — check the Recycle Bin"; the Kit's refusal messages are shown as the Kit's spec words them. Tables, the look of their columns (Windows, ASP-22; owner, so that neighbouring columns are easy to tell apart): in every table (Large and old, Temporary files, Duplicates, Uninstall, Apps and caches, History, the largest files) **the columns alternate**. Normal theme: every other column, counting the first (Move or Name) as the first, has a band a little lighter than the ground behind it, from its header cell down through every row; the band is a theme resource (`LeanBand` in Theme.xaml), faint enough to show a row's hover and selection through it, and it never covers a tick box, the focus ring or the text. High Contrast: Windows allows only its own colours, and the window and button-face colours can be the same, so there is no band; a 1 px vertical divider in the system disabled-text colour (GrayText) runs between every two columns, from the header to the last row. The one exception is the Duplicates groups, whose rows keep their own alternating grounds (exactly LeanPanel or LeanGround, ASP-15): they have neither band nor divider. The divider and the band are decoration and not part of the accessibility tree.
